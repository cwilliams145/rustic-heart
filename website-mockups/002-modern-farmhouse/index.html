The Baker

Meet Debbie, the heart
behind the stand

Hi there! I'm Debbie Crick. What started as a love for canning and preserving has grown into a little roadside spot on Cornville Road, full of homemade pies, cakes, cookies, and all kinds of treats made from scratch.

Everything here is baked with care using real ingredients — no shortcuts, no preservatives, just good old-fashioned homemade yummies. Come grab a sample, chat a while, and take home something delicious.

— Debbie
