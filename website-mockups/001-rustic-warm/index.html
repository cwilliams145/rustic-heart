Meet Debbie

The heart behind The Rustic Heart

Hi there! I'm Debbie Crick, and I've been baking up homemade goodness from my farmstand on Cornville Road for years. What started as a love for canning and preserving has grown into a little roadside spot full of pies, cakes, cookies, and all kinds of treats made from scratch.

Everything you'll find here is baked with care using real ingredients — no shortcuts, no preservatives, just good old-fashioned homemade yummies. Come grab a sample, chat a while, and take home something tasty.

— Debbie 💛
